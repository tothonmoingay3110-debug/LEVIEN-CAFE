# V2.11 — Staff operations suite

V2.11 delivers the planned V2.6–V2.11 staff upgrades as one cumulative release.
It builds on individual staff identities, published schedules, labor planning,
time off, and weekly schedule copying from V2.1A–V2.5.

## Included

### V2.6 — In-app notifications

- Private notifications for schedule creation, updates, cancellation and copied weeks.
- Coverage and time-off review notifications.
- Per-user unread count, individual read action, and Mark all read.
- In-app delivery only. There is no email or SMS integration.

### V2.7 — Shift coverage

- An employee can offer one of their own future published shifts to an eligible coworker.
- The original assignment stays unchanged until a manager approves the request.
- Approval re-checks active status, approved time off and overlapping shifts.
- A Manager cannot review their own request or a request involving an Owner account.
- Every request can be approved, declined or cancelled only through an authorized action.

### V2.8 — My Workspace

- Upcoming personal shifts and scheduled-hour total.
- Upcoming personal time-off summary.
- Private notification inbox.
- Personal and team coverage requests according to role.
- Compensation and hourly pay are never returned by this workspace API.

### V2.9 — Staff reports

- Owner/Manager-only weekly, monthly or custom date ranges up to 93 days.
- Published shift count, scheduled days and scheduled hours.
- Approved time-off days and coverage-request totals.
- CSV export generated locally in the browser.
- Reports describe the schedule only; they are not attendance or payroll reports.

### V2.10 — Activity history

- Owner/Manager-only append-only application log.
- Records who created, changed, cancelled or copied schedules.
- Records coverage and time-off requests/reviews.
- Search and entity filters over the latest 200 events.

### V2.11 — Production and responsive hardening

- Server-side authorization on every new endpoint.
- Same-origin and body-size protection on mutations.
- RLS enabled on all new tables; browser roles receive no direct table privileges.
- Mobile layouts for workspace cards, reports, tables, filters and activity history.
- Grouped accordion navigation keeps related modules together; mobile uses a compact
  group row with horizontally scrollable sub-navigation.
- Existing storefront, checkout, orders and catalog behavior are unchanged.

## Role access

| Capability | Owner | Manager | Supervisor | Staff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Own workspace and notifications | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Offer own future shift | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Review team coverage | Yes | Yes, except own/Owner | No | No |
| Staff reports and CSV | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Activity Log | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Hourly pay / labor cost | Yes | Yes | No | No |

## Database rollout

V2.1A, V2.1B, V2.2 and V2.4 must already be installed. V2.3 and V2.5 did not
add migrations. In Supabase Dashboard → SQL Editor, run:

```text
supabase/migrations/20260822000200_v2_11_staff_operations.sql
```

The migration creates:

- `staff_notifications`
- `staff_shift_swap_requests`
- `staff_audit_log`
- `shift_swap_status`

It is safe to run the migration once on the project used by the application's
`NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL`. Keep the service-role key server-only.

## Verification

1. Sign in as Staff and open My Workspace.
2. Confirm upcoming shifts appear without compensation values.
3. Offer a future shift to an active coworker.
4. Sign in as Manager and approve the request.
5. Confirm the shift moves to the coworker and both employees receive a notification.
6. Create, edit and cancel a test future shift; confirm Activity Log records each action.
7. Open Staff Reports, choose This month and export the CSV.
8. Confirm Supervisor/Staff cannot open Staff Reports or Activity Log.
9. Test the admin workspace below 720 px wide.

No clock-in, attendance, payroll, email or SMS data is introduced by this release.
