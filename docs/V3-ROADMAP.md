# V3 — Reporting & Business Intelligence

| Module | Scope | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Report Center | Sales, Orders, Customers, Products, Promotions, Combos, CSV and Excel export | Complete |
| 2. KPI Dashboard | Revenue, orders, AOV, best/low sellers, customer mix and seven-day outlook | Complete |
| 3. Customer Analytics | History, frequency, spending, favorites, returning customers and segments | Complete |
| 4. Product Analytics | Best sellers, slow movers, revenue, quantity, toppings and combos | Complete |
| 5. Promotion & Combo Analytics | Impressions, clicks, attributed orders/revenue, combo sales and customer savings | Complete |
| 6. POS Import Module | CSV/Excel import, validation, mapping and sales history | Skipped until a real POS export format is available |
| 7. AI Business Insights | Slow-product detection, combo/promotion ideas, segments, trends and basic forecast | Complete |

The POS module remains intentionally unlocked until a real export sample is
available. Tax and advanced accounting details are not part of V3 unless the
business requests them later. Combo profitability is also deferred until real
product-cost data exists; V3 shows sales and customer savings without inventing
a margin.
