# Sprint 4.4.1 — Combo Builder Polish

- Admin combo builder calculates regular price automatically.
- Shows combo price and customer savings in real time.
- Requires a combo name, at least two products, and a positive combo price.
- Prevents saving a combo price equal to or above the regular total.
- Displays product prices, categories, sold-out state, and selected item summary.
- Combo list shows regular total, combo price, and savings.
