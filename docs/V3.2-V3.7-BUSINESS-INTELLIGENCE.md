# V3.2–V3.7 — Reporting & Business Intelligence

This cumulative release completes V3 Modules 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7. Module 6 POS
Import is intentionally skipped until LEVIEN has a real POS export sample.

## Access

Owner and Manager can open these pages under `Admin → Planning & Reports`:

- KPI Dashboard
- Customer Analytics
- Product Analytics
- Promotion & Combo Analytics
- AI Business Insights

Supervisor and Staff cannot open the pages or call the protected API. Every
request is verified server-side with `view_sales_reports`.

Every analytics page exports its complete selected date range as UTF-8 CSV or
an Excel-compatible `.xls` workbook. Exported customer files are restricted to
the same Owner/Manager permission as the on-screen report.

## Shared reporting rules

- Sales include only orders with status `Completed`.
- Revenue is completed-order subtotal after a loyalty discount.
- Tax, delivery fees and advanced accounting are intentionally excluded.
- Date ranges are interpreted in the LEVIEN store timezone
  (`America/New_York`) and may cover up to 366 days.
- Gift Card payments are tender, not a sales discount.

## Module 2 — KPI Dashboard

Shows revenue, completed orders, average order value, items sold, best and low
sellers, new and returning customer counts, active promotions, daily revenue,
and a transparent seven-day run-rate outlook.

## Module 3 — Customer Analytics

Customers are linked using their customer account when available, with the
normalized checkout phone as the guest fallback. The page shows range orders,
spend, average order, favorite product, average purchase frequency, first/last
orders, and four practical segments:

- **New:** first completed purchase falls inside the selected range.
- **Returning:** purchased before the selected range.
- **Loyal:** at least five lifetime completed orders.
- **VIP:** at least fifteen lifetime completed orders or $300 lifetime spend.

## Module 4 — Product Analytics

Standalone purchases and products included in combos both count toward product
demand. Standalone revenue stays separate because allocating a combo's price
across products without an agreed business rule would create misleading data.
The module also measures topping quantity, revenue and order reach.

## Module 5 — Promotion & Combo Analytics

Homepage promotions now record privacy-conscious impressions and clicks. A
promotion click is held in the browser session for up to seven days and linked
to the next created order when that promotion is still active. Reports show:

- impressions, clicks and click-through rate;
- attributed completed orders and subtotal revenue;
- combo units, orders and revenue;
- regular separate price, combo price and total customer savings.

The random promotion session key contains no name, email, phone, tracking token,
IP address or customer ID. Tracking failures never block menu navigation or
checkout.

True combo profitability is not displayed because product cost data does not
exist yet. The system does not guess a margin.

## Module 7 — AI Business Insights

Insights are deterministic and explainable. The engine compares recent sales
halves, ranks slow products, proposes a best-seller/slow-mover pairing, checks
promotion engagement, summarizes customer segments, and calculates a basic
seven-day forecast from average daily revenue and order volume.

No external AI service receives customer or order data, and no paid AI key is
required. Every recommendation includes evidence and a suggested next action.

## Database rollout

Run this migration once in Supabase SQL Editor:

`supabase/migrations/20260825000100_v3_business_intelligence.sql`

It adds nullable `orders.promotion_id` attribution and the protected
`promotion_events` table. Existing orders remain valid and receive no promotion
unless they are created after a tracked homepage click.

## Verification

1. Run `npx.cmd tsc --noEmit`.
2. Run `npm.cmd run build -- --webpack`.
3. Apply the migration, then restart/redeploy the application.
4. Sign in as Owner or Manager and open all five V3 analytics pages.
5. Change the range and confirm every page refreshes.
6. Open the homepage promotion, click through, place an order, mark it
   Completed, and confirm attribution in Promotion & Combo Analytics.
7. Confirm Supervisor and Staff cannot access the analytics API.

Historical orders created before this migration still contribute to sales,
customer, product and combo analytics. They simply have no promotion
attribution.
