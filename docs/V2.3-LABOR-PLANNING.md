# V2.3 — Labor planning

V2.3 gives Owner and Manager a private weekly labor forecast built from employee
compensation and published work shifts. It does not calculate payroll, taxes,
overtime rules, attendance, or clocked hours.

## Included

- Planned weekly hours from each employee profile.
- Scheduled hours calculated from V2.2 published work shifts.
- Planned-vs-scheduled variance for each employee.
- Planned labor cost (`planned hours × hourly rate`).
- Forecast labor cost (`scheduled hours × hourly rate`).
- Daily scheduled hours, shift count, employee coverage, and forecast cost.
- Weekly navigation and employee search.
- UTF-8 CSV export for management reporting.

## Permissions

| Action | Owner | Manager | Supervisor | Staff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open Labor Planning | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| View hourly rate | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| View labor forecast | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Export labor CSV | Yes | Yes | No | No |

Navigation is role-aware, and `/api/admin/labor` independently enforces the
`view_compensation` permission. Hiding the page in the browser is not the only
security control.

## Data sources

- `staff_profiles`: employee name, role, and account state.
- `staff_compensation`: planned weekly hours and hourly rate.
- `staff_shifts`: official scheduled shifts with `scheduled` status.

Cancelled shifts and preferred shift requests are excluded from the forecast.
Locked employees remain visible for historical clarity when they have schedule
data or compensation configured.

## How to use

1. Sign in to `/admin` as Owner or Manager.
2. Open **Labor Planning**.
3. Choose the week with Previous, This week, or Next.
4. Review total planned hours, scheduled hours, forecast cost, and variance.
5. Use the employee table to find over- or under-scheduled employees.
6. Choose **Export CSV** when a management snapshot is needed.

## Verification checklist

- Owner and Manager can load the report.
- Supervisor and Staff cannot see the navigation item.
- Direct unauthorized API requests return 403 without compensation data.
- Scheduled hours match the sum of published shift durations.
- Cancelled shifts do not affect totals.
- Employee forecast equals scheduled hours multiplied by hourly rate.
- CSV totals match the on-screen report.
- Changing a schedule or compensation value updates the report after refresh.

## Important limitation

The forecast is a planning estimate before taxes, breaks, overtime premiums,
tips, bonuses, deductions, and payroll adjustments. It must not be treated as a
final paycheck or legal payroll record.
