# Sprint 4.4 — Fixed Combo Engine

## Included

- Combo remains a separate module from products.
- Admin selects the fixed products included in a combo.
- Combo has its own price.
- Customers customize each included product separately.
- Topping charges are added on top of the combo price.
- Cart, checkout, tracking and Admin Orders keep the combo grouped.
- A combo becomes unavailable when an included product is missing or sold out.

## Deferred

- Choose-one-from-a-group combos.
- Product substitutions and upcharges.
- Time-based combo availability.
- Smart combo suggestions.
